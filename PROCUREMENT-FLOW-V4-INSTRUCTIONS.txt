PROCUREMENT WORKFLOW V4
=======================

Purpose
-------
Models the KASU Procurement Directorate/PMU structure only for the part of the project that is in scope:
market survey, price adjustment, BOQ preparation, submission to VC, and post-VC processing.
It does NOT implement tender execution, vendor award, delivery, Stores, GRN, or Bursary payment.

Workflow
--------
HOD -> DEAN -> PROVOST -> PROCUREMENT INTAKE/ASSIGNMENT -> MARKET SURVEY -> VC -> PROCUREMENT PROCESSING

Procurement staff positions
---------------------------
- Director of Procurement
- Principal / Senior Procurement Officer
- Procurement Officer I
- Procurement Officer II
- Administrative / Clerical Officer (defined for structure, not used for market survey)

Important setup
---------------
1. Log in as System Administrator.
2. Go to Users.
3. Edit one Procurement account and set its Procurement position to:
   Director of Procurement.
4. Edit/create the staff who will actually conduct market surveys and set them to:
   Principal / Senior Procurement Officer, Procurement Officer I, or Procurement Officer II.
5. Log out and log back in as Procurement after changing the position so the client session receives the new position.

How assignment works
--------------------
- After Provost approval, the requisition first lands with the Director of Procurement.
- The Director (or Principal/Senior when authorized) assigns the market-survey work to a Principal/Senior or Procurement Officer I/II.
- The assigned officer edits market-survey unit costs, records reasons/notes, generates the BOQ and submits to VC.
- The same assigned officer is automatically set as the post-VC Procurement processing officer.
- VC approval returns the requisition to that same officer for processing.

Backward compatibility
----------------------
Existing Procurement users without procurementPosition are given the Mongoose default Procurement Officer II when loaded.
If no Director account is configured, routing falls back to Principal/Senior, then another active Procurement account so existing installations do not immediately stop working.
For the intended KASU workflow, configure a Director account before testing new requisitions.

Testing sequence
----------------
1. Create a normal requisition.
2. HOD approves.
3. Dean approves.
4. Provost approves.
5. Log in as Director of Procurement.
6. Confirm the requisition appears in Procurement Intake.
7. Open it and assign a Procurement Officer I/II (or Principal/Senior).
8. Log in as the assigned officer.
9. Confirm it appears in My Market Survey Queue.
10. Change every market-survey unit cost and enter reasons.
11. Save the market survey.
12. Generate/view the BOQ.
13. Submit to VC.
14. Log in as VC and confirm the revised prices and BOQ are visible.
15. VC approves.
16. Log in as the assigned Procurement officer again.
17. Confirm the requisition is in Ready for Processing.
